SkyDek DJ 0.10.0 adds a redesigned settings sidebar, deck playback and end-of-track lighting, reduced motion, and a USB MIDI Controllers tab with saved profiles and guided MIDI Learn.

Also included since 0.9.1: Auto DJ, Dark/Light/Xbox skins, transparent in-app logo artwork and simplified sampler settings.

Windows x64 preview. USB MIDI Note and absolute CC are supported. Physical hardware testing is pending. The Windows ZIP includes the installer, guide, third-party notices, checksums and complete source for the separate MP3 encoding utility.
